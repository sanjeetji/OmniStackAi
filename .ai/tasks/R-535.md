# R-535 — CareClinic (Part 1 of 4): Clinical Care & Telemedicine API, Database & Seed

## Context & Objective
Start the CareClinic clinical care and telemedicine platform template (Tracker ID T-8, category: `healthcare`):
1. Scaffold hidden draft template at `templates/catalog/_care-clinic`.
2. Write root `template.json` draft manifest with 4 apps (`patient`, `doctor`, `admin`, `api`), 4 roles (`patient`, `doctor`, `receptionist`, `admin`), 18 entities, 10 features, 3 demo users (`ananya@careclinic.test`, `dr.rajesh@careclinic.test`, `admin@careclinic.test`), and 48 screen definitions.
3. Build `services/api`:
   - 5 complete PostgreSQL migrations:
     - `001_core.sql`: users, clinics, doctor_profiles, patient_profiles, family_members.
     - `002_scheduling.sql`: availability rules, overrides, appointments, slot uniqueness constraint.
     - `003_clinical.sql`: medical history, vitals, SOAP consultations, ICD-10 diagnoses, immutable e-prescriptions, lab catalog, orders, and results.
     - `004_billing.sql`: invoices, consultation fees, refunds.
     - `005_audit_telehealth.sql`: chart access audit logs, telehealth WebRTC sessions, patient reviews, notifications.
   - Core TypeScript Hono application with config, db connection pool, and JWT authentication.
   - Clinical domain services:
     - Scheduling & dynamic slot generator (zero double-booking).
     - Clinical notes & immutable prescriptions.
     - Patient record access control & chart audit logging.
     - Billing, cancellation refund rules.
     - Telehealth session manager.
   - Route handlers: `/public`, `/patient`, `/doctor`, `/admin`, `/stream` (SSE).
   - Deterministic seed generator `scripts/generate-seed.mjs` producing `seed/001_demo.sql` (12 doctors across 8 specialties, 150 patients, 900 appointments, consultations, prescriptions, lab reports, billing).
   - Automated unit tests covering auth, slot generation, appointment state machine, and chart security.
4. Verify studio template tests and `task verify`.
