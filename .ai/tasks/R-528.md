# R-528: RideNow template, part 3 of 4: driver app (PWA) (Phase T, T-6)

**Phase:** Phase T (Template Marketplace)
**Status:** COMPLETED (2026-09-22)
**Started:** 2026-09-22

## Objective

`apps/driver` is the RideNow Driver app: mobile-first, installable (web app manifest and icons),
shown in a phone frame in the preview. It covers everything a driver does on a shift. The page
targets are in `.ai/tasks/R-526.md`.

## Design

- **Stack:** the same as the rider app (Next 16, React 19, Tailwind 4, `@ridenow/shared`).
- **Look:** dark and high-contrast for night driving. Near-black surfaces, "online" green and
  amber for offers and surge, large touch targets, the Sora typeface. Its own design tokens, so
  it looks nothing like the rider app.
- **Location:** in the preview the computer's GPS is not in Bengaluru, so the app offers two modes:
  - **Simulated** (default in the preview): start at your last position, move along the route
    during a trip.
  - **Device GPS**: `navigator.geolocation`.

  Either way the app posts `/driver/location` about every 3 s while online, which is what keeps the
  rider's live map and dispatch working. The simulation is labelled as such in the UI.
- **Pages:**
  - `/login` and `/apply`: sign in, or apply as a new driver (vehicle details). New drivers see
    "pending approval" until ops approve them.
  - `/`: home. Online/offline switch (going online needs approval and a location), a map with
    surge zones and my position, today's earnings, trips, acceptance and rating, and a pending
    payout nudge.
  - Incoming request sheet (on every page): fare, my earning, pickup distance, trip length, rider
    rating, payment method, a countdown ring to expiry, accept or decline. It arrives over SSE,
    with a poll fallback.
  - `/trip`: the active trip. Head to pickup (map, rider name, call, "arrived"), enter the PIN,
    on trip (drop, "complete"), then done (cash to collect or "paid by wallet", your earning,
    rate the rider). Cancel with a reason before the ride starts.
  - `/earnings`: today, week or month. Totals, per-day bar chart (SVG), trips, online time, cash
    collected and 5-star count.
  - `/wallet`: balance, withdraw (minimum ₹100, one pending at a time), payouts and ledger.
  - `/trips` and `/trips/[id]`: history and detail (earning breakdown, rating received).
  - `/ratings`: average, star breakdown, top compliments, recent comments.
  - `/account`: profile, vehicle, document status (with expiry warnings), sign out.
  - `/help` and `/help/[id]`, `/notifications`.
- **Navigation:** a bottom tab bar (Home, Earnings, Trips, Account) with an "online" status pill.

## Gates

tsc and `next build` (scratch install). The preview with api, rider and driver: every driver page
returns 200 through the proxy, and the PWA manifest is served. A live cross-app journey through the
proxy: the driver goes online, a rider books, the offer reaches the driver, accept, arrive, PIN,
complete, and the money settles. Then `task verify`, `task lint`, `task security:quick`,
`task env:check` and `./scripts/smoke-core.sh`.

  All passed.
- **Offline:** `test_template_ride_now.py` 6/6. The new `RideNowAppsTests` checks the driver pages
  and PWA files, and cross-checks every API path named in the rider and driver code against the

  `task security:quick`, `task env:check`, and `./scripts/smoke-core.sh` "Core loop passed".

  - The home page called `/driver/zones`, which the API did not have. The endpoint was added, with

  - Nested dynamic pages lost the "· RideNow" title suffix in both apps: the parent layouts'
    plain-string titles reset the template.
  - Wallet labels and payout statuses did not match the database (`rejected`, not `failed`).
  - Trip timeline labels did not match the recorded event kinds.
  - Rating tips did not match the tags riders can pick.
- **Not done here:** screenshots. Headless Chrome on this machine cannot capture signed-in pages:

  listing and must solve this.
- **Editor note:** the draft folder has no `node_modules` (the template validator forbids them), so
