## Objective
Provide an accessible, production-grade, zero-dependency, and futuristic **Speed Dial & Floating Action Button (FAB)** component suite (`apps/web/components/speed-dial.tsx`) in Next.js web applications generated by OmniStackAI.
Features:
- Standalone compound suite: `SpeedDial` (with compound aliases `SpeedDial.Trigger`, `SpeedDial.Action`, `SpeedDial.Content`).
- Exports TypeScript interfaces: `SpeedDialDirection` (`"up"` | `"down"` | `"left"` | `"right"`), `SpeedDialVariant` (`"neon"` | `"glass"` | `"bordered"` | `"minimal"`), `SpeedDialSize` (`"sm"` | `"md"` | `"lg"`), `SpeedDialActionItem`, `SpeedDialProps`, `SpeedDialTriggerProps`, `SpeedDialActionProps`, `SpeedDialContentProps`, `SpeedDialContextValue`.
- Primary Floating Action Button with animated icon transition (e.g. `+` morphs/rotates 45° to `×` on open).
- Directional action cascade (`direction="up"`, `"down"`, `"left"`, `"right"`) with staggered entrance/exit transitions.
- Action item labels/tooltips with accessible screen-reader support.
- Backdrop overlay option (`backdrop?: boolean`) with subtle blur and click-to-dismiss.
- Click-outside detection and auto-close when clicking outside.
- Controlled and uncontrolled open state management (`open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`).
- Full WAI-ARIA 1.2 Menu semantics (`role="menu"`, `role="menuitem"`, `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded`).
- Full keyboard navigation (`Escape` closes speed dial, `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` or `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` cycles through items, `Home`/`End`, `Enter`/`Space` activates).
- 4 futuristic visual variants: `"neon"` (cyberpunk glowing border and cyan pulse glow), `"glass"` (translucent frosted backdrop blur), `"bordered"` (clean slate border frame), and `"minimal"` (flat circular button).
- 3 size presets: `"sm"`, `"md"`, `"lg"`.
- 100% diff-invariant across `ir.description`; zero external npm packages.
