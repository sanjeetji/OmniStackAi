## Objective
Add an accessible, desktop-grade, zero-dependency, and futuristic Scroll Area / Custom Viewport compound component suite (`apps/web/components/scroll-area.tsx`) in the OmniStackAI Next.js application generator (`services/agent-engine`).

## Requirements & Acceptance Criteria
1. **Component Emitted**: `NextjsWebAdapter.generate()` emits `apps/web/components/scroll-area.tsx` in all generated Next.js web applications. (Verified: `builder:demo` emits `components/scroll-area.tsx` across minimal-blog and rideshare-favourites).
2. **Codegen Helper**: `omnistackai_agent_engine.codegen` exports `render_scroll_area_component()`. (Verified in `codegen/__init__.py`).
3. **Compound Architecture**:
   - `ScrollArea`: Root container managing layout, orientation, visibility modes, and variant styling.
   - `ScrollArea.Viewport`: Internal scrollable container with native scrollbars visually hidden across browsers.
   - `ScrollArea.Scrollbar`: Floating track for vertical and horizontal orientations.
   - `ScrollArea.Thumb`: Proportional draggable thumb element.
   - `ScrollArea.Corner`: Square rendered at the intersection when both scrollbars are active.
   - `useScrollArea()`: Context hook exposing scroll coordinates and programmatic scroll helpers (`scrollToTop`, `scrollToBottom`, `scrollTo`).
4. **Visibility Modes**:
   - `"auto"`: Shows when content overflows, auto-hides when idle.
   - `"always"`: Permanently visible.
   - `"scroll"`: Shows strictly during active scrolling, auto-hiding after delay.
   - `"hover"`: Shows when hovering the scroll area container.
5. **Interactive Physics**:
   - Mouse and touch dragging recalculates viewport scroll position smoothly.
   - Proportional thumb sizing based on viewport to content ratio.
   - Track pointer clicks navigate towards clicked location.
6. **Visual Variants & Sizes**:
   - 4 variants: `"neon"`, `"glass"`, `"bordered"`, `"minimal"`.
   - 3 size presets: `"sm"` (4px), `"md"` (8px), `"lg"` (12px).
7. **Accessibility**:
   - `role="scrollbar"`, `aria-orientation`, `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-controls` on scrollbar track.
   - `tabIndex={0}` and keyboard navigation on viewport (`ArrowDown`/`ArrowUp`, `PageDown`/`PageUp`, `Home`, `End`).
8. **Diff Invariance & Independence**:
   - Pure React (zero third-party npm packages).
   - 100% diff-invariant across `ir.description`.
