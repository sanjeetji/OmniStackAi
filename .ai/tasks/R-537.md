# R-537 — CareClinic (Part 3 of 4): Doctor Telehealth & Clinical Workstation

## Context & Objective
Build the physician workstation for CareClinic (Tracker ID T-8, category: `healthcare`):
- `apps/doctor`:
  - Framework: Next.js 16 App Router + React 19 + Turbopack + Tailwind CSS 4.
  - Design aesthetic: High-efficiency clinical workstation styling — modern slate/navy foundations (`#0F172A`), clinical emerald/cyan accents (`#059669` / `#0284c7`), high data density, keyboard-first clinical shortcuts, accessible contrast.
  - Demo User Quick Login: Dr. Rajesh Varma, MD (`dr.rajesh@careclinic.test` / `Doctor@2026`).
  - 14 routes matching `templates/catalog/_care-clinic/template.json`:
    - `/login`: Doctor Sign In with 1-click Dr. Rajesh Varma demo credentials.
    - `/`: Clinical Dashboard with OPD metrics (Today's scheduled visits, active queue tokens, completed consults, revenue collected, urgent lab reports).
    - `/queue`: Today's Patient Queue with live tokens 1-15, arrival status, wait times, chief complaints, and 1-click "Call Patient / Start Consult".
    - `/consult/[appointmentId]`: Active Consultation Workspace (patient banner, vitals summary, quick action tabs).
    - `/consult/[appointmentId]/soap`: Structured SOAP Documentation Editor (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) with ICD-10 search and finalization lock.
    - `/consult/[appointmentId]/prescription`: Digital Prescription Builder (itemized medication list: form, dose, frequency, route, timing, duration, instructions, and tamper-proof digital signature generator).
    - `/consult/[appointmentId]/labs`: Diagnostic Lab Order Generator (multi-test catalog selector, clinical indication notes, urgent turnaround flag).
    - `/patients/[id]`: Longitudinal Patient Health Chart (timeline of previous consults, historical vitals trends, previous prescriptions, and HIPAA-compliant access audit log badge).
    - `/patients/[id]/history`: Comprehensive Medical History (chronic conditions, drug allergies, past surgeries, family history).
    - `/patients`: Patient Medical Directory & Longitudinal Index with search and chronic condition filters.
    - `/schedule`: Schedule Configuration & Matrix (weekly OPD days, shift start/end hours, slot duration: 15/20/30 mins).
    - `/schedule/rules`: Availability Rules & Leave Overrides (capacity limits, tariffs, emergency leave blocker).
    - `/earnings`: Consultation Revenue & Payouts (daily/monthly earnings, in-clinic vs video consult breakdown, settlement batch status).
    - `/reviews`: Patient Feedback & Ratings (verified reviews, star ratings, patient comments).
    - `/telehealth/[appointmentId]`: Telehealth Doctor Call Station (video consult room with patient camera feed, in-call SOAP note taking, live chat, consultation timer).
- Extended `@careclinic/shared` with doctor SDK methods (`getDoctorQueue`, `startConsultation`, `saveSoapNotes`, `finalizeConsultation`, `issuePrescription`, `createLabOrder`, `getPatientChart`, `getDoctorSchedule`, `getDoctorEarnings`, `getDoctorReviews`).
